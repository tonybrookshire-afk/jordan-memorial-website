About Us

Get to know Jordan Memorial Baptist Church, formerly Jordan Street Baptist Church.

Our History

Our roots go back to 1899, when D. A. P. Jordan moved Greenwood’s former First Presbyterian Church building to Jordan Street. It served as a Union Chapel for Baptists and Methodists in the Grendel community.

The congregation organized as Jordan Street Baptist Church in 1906 and erected its first church building at Jordan Street and Smythe Avenue in 1910.

In 1978, the congregation voted to call the new church Jordan Memorial Baptist Church and broke ground on East Durst Avenue on December 3. The first service in the new building took place on December 23, 1979, and the building was dedicated on June 8, 1980.

The congregation marked 100 years of ministry in 2006. Today, we gather for worship at 1515 Durst Avenue East in Greenwood.

Source: Jordan Memorial Baptist Church, Celebrating 100 Years of Ministry: Historical Highlights, pp. 1–4.
